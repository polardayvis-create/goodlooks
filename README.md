goodlooks 🐻

Good people. Good businesses. Good opportunities.

goodlooks is a Massachusetts-focused community app designed to help people:

- 📍 Find local businesses using Whitepages and Yellow Pages.
- 💼 Discover job opportunities.
- 💰 Find tax information and resources.
- 🏪 Promote local businesses.
- 🤝 Connect with community resources.

Our Mission

Help Massachusetts residents discover local businesses and useful opportunities.

Future Plans

- Business advertising submissions
- Local business directory
- More community resources
- Android app publishing on Google Play
